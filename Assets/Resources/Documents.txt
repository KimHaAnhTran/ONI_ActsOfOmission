Open Door Policy in China Prevent serious conflicts of interest among world powers in the Orient, ensure American business and citizen rights in the region. No nation should get a head start on commerce and navigation. IF, following success in the Philippines, we must secure our piece of the Chinese market, the most coveted prize in Asia.
We could not give Cuba back to Spain. We could not turn the Filipinos over to France or Germany, our commercial rivals in the Orient. We could not leave them to themselves, they were unfit for self-governance. We, therefore, must educate them, uplift, civilize, and Christianize them.
Some Filipino tribesmen have horribly mutilated our captured soldiers. Morale is low. We have built concentration camps to keep order among our prisoners.
We made war on Spain four years ago for doing the very things of which we are now guilty ourselves. We are giving Spain as good reason to interfere with us on the ground of humanity as we had to interfere with her. Doubtless she would interfere if she were strong enough and thought she could acquire some islands in the virtuous act.
