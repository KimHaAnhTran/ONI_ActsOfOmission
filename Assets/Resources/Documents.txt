The western frontier is closed. Our national energy has reached its physical limit. To prevent economic stagnation, I recommend expansion to other territories. We look outward, or we cease to grow.
The true measure of a nation's greatness is in its sea power through maritime industry and adventurous commerce. To sustain this influence in both war and peace, the state must have a vigorous navy. Yet, a fleet without overseas bases is like a land bird, tethered to its own coast. Therefore, the acquisition of coaling stations in foreign waters is a paramount duty to ensure the navy can protect trade and prevent blockades.

We propose an Open Door Policy in China for the following reasons: Prevent serious conflicts of interest among world powers in the Orient, ensure American business and citizen rights in the region. No nation should get a head start on treatment of commerce and navigation. The Open Door Policy is therefore equal. IF, following success in the Philippines, we must secure our piece of the Chinese market, the most coveted prize in Asia.
I would like to say just a word about the Philippine and Cuban business: We could not give Cuba back to Spain, that would be cowardly and dishonorable. We could not turn the Filipinos over to France or Germany, our commercial rivals in the Orient. We could not leave them to themselves, they were unfit for self-governance. We, therefore, must educate them, uplift, civilize, and Christianize them. The Philippines will stay on the map of the United States. And there it will stay while I am President!
Some Filipino tribesmen have horribly mutilated our captured soldiers. Morale is low. We have built concentration camps to keep order among our prisoners.
We made war on Spain four years ago for doing the very things of which we are now guilty ourselves. We are giving Spain as good reason to interfere with us on the ground of humanity as we had to interfere with her. Doubtless she would interfere if she were strong enough and thought she could acquire some islands in the virtuous act.

WAR ERUPTS IN EUROPE. President Wilson’s heart goes out to suffering Allies. Congress retains neutrality. "NEUTRAL" INTERVENTION? Major American bank J.P. Morgan has loaned over $2 billion to the Allies war effort in Europe. 
BARBARIC GERMAN U-BOAT SANK RMS LUSITANIA. 128 AMERICANS DEAD. The public cries outrage! IS WAR INEVITABLE? Intercepted German telegraph reveals a proposal to Mexico: declare war on the United States, then Germany will help them reclaim the American Southwest.
To suggest we must enter this European war for the sake of morality is the height of hypocrisy. Let us not forget the occupation of Veracruz. We claimed to invade to "protect democracy," yet no news has come out of this "German ship" unloading ammunition on Mexican shores. Are we so blind as to not see this as a maneuver to undermine a government that refused to bow to American interests? Heed Washington's words: let us not intervene in foreign affairs! We must not sacrifice more American lives.
